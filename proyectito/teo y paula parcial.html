Welcome to the Astroscope!

Introduction

Have you ever looked up and wondered what that bright dot is? Astroscope tells you exactly what you’re looking at whether it’s visible tonight, where to look, and which constellation it belongs to. Search for any planet, star, or galaxy and get real time visibility information in seconds. And if you want to look for something more specific, you can click the icons on the navigation bar to access a section dedicated exclusively to that type of celestial body.

Discover the Universe

The night sky is always changing. Planets drift slowly against the background stars, the Moon cycles through its phases every month, and different constellations rise and set with the seasons. What you can see and when depends on your location, the time of night, and how dark the sky. Astroscope keeps track of all of that for you, so instead of guessing where to look, you'll know exactly which direction to face and what you're about to discover.
