Black holes

A planet reflects light. A black hole erases it. Nothing that crosses the event horizon — not gas, not light, not information — ever comes back out. Below are five, ordered by mass: from a stellar corpse twenty times heavier than the Sun, to an object that outweighs every star in the Milky Way combined.

Cygnus X-1

The first object widely accepted by astronomers as a black hole. It orbits a blue supergiant star, tearing gas from it into a disk so hot it glows in X-rays.
