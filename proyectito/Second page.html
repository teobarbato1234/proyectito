Planets

Mercury

The smallest planet and the closest to the Sun. It has almost no atmosphere and huge temperature swings between day and night.

Venus

Similar in size to Earth, but covered in thick toxic clouds. It's the hottest planet due to a powerful greenhouse effect.

Earth

The only known planet with life. It has liquid water, a breathable atmosphere, and one natural satellite: the Moon.

Mars

Known as the Red Planet because of its iron oxide surface. Home to the largest volcano and canyon in the Solar System.

Jupiter

The largest planet in the Solar System, a gas giant famous for its Great Red Spot, a giant storm bigger than Earth.

Saturn

Best known for its spectacular ring system made of ice and rock. It's the least dense planet, less dense than water.

Uranus

An ice giant that spins almost on its side. It has a pale blue color due to methane in its atmosphere.

Neptune

The farthest planet from the Sun, a windy ice giant with the fastest winds in the Solar System.
